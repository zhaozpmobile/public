Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Take turns placing pieces on the 5x5 board. Forming certain patterns along the board's edges or as a 2x2 square captures your opponent's pieces. Once the board is full, you'll take turns moving your pieces one step at a time (up, down, left, or right — no diagonal moves), still trying to form capturing patterns. Reduce your opponent to 2 pieces to win.

Is there a way to play against another person?
Yes — switch to "Two Players" mode to pass the device back and forth with a friend on the same iPhone.

Can I adjust how strong the AI is?
Yes — in Single Player mode, choose Easy, Medium, or Hard from the difficulty selector above the board.

Does the app track me or show personalized ads?
No. Borderline shows non-personalized ads only and does not use your Advertising Identifier or track your activity. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?
