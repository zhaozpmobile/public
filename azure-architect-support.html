Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All content is stored in the app and works fully offline.

Is the app free? Are there ads?

The app and all 22 sections are free, with no ads and no account required.

What data does the app collect?

None. See the Privacy Policy.

How does search work?

Type in the search box to search every section at once. Results show the section, the heading, and a preview with your words highlighted. Tap a result to jump to that heading. Results under “Related” match some of your words but not all of them.

How do I move around a long section?

Use the Contents button in the top-right corner to jump to any heading in the section you are reading.

Tables or diagrams look cut off.

Wide tables and diagrams scroll sideways. Swipe left and right on them (or use two fingers on a Mac trackpad).

When was the content last updated?

Open the Info button in the sidebar to see the “Content updated” date.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and Mac with macOS 14 or later.

Is this an official Microsoft app?

No. Cloud Architect Pro is an independent study guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. Azure services change often, so please confirm details in the official documentation at learn.microsoft.com.
