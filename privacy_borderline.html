Privacy Policy for Borderline: A Capture Game

Last updated: 8/18/2026

Borderline: A Capture Game ("the App," "we," "us") is a single-player and local two-player board game for iOS. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices — in plain language.

Summary

The App does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or any personal profile information, and does not track you across other apps or websites. The only data handling involved comes from displaying non-personalized advertising through Google AdMob, and a small amount of gameplay data (win/loss counts) stored only on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required, so we never collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because we do not track you.

Information Stored On Your Device Only

The App keeps a simple win/loss counter for your games, stored locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device, is not sent to us or to any third party, and is deleted automatically if you delete the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads through Google's AdMob service to keep the App free. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized — we do not request or use your Advertising Identifier, and ads are not targeted based on your personal profile or browsing history.

To serve any ad at all, however, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process some technical information as part of the ad request itself, such as:

  • A general, IP-based estimate of region (not precise location)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Basic diagnostic and crash data, to keep the ad service working correctly

This information is processed by Google, not by us directly, and is not linked to your identity. For details on how Google's advertising products handle data, see Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use AdMob

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share any information with third parties ourselves, beyond the ad-serving process described above, which is handled directly between your device and Google's Mobile Ads SDK.

Your Choices

Since the App does not create a profile of you or track you across other apps or services, there is no personalized account data to request, export, or delete beyond the local win/loss counter already described — which you can clear at any time by deleting the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features that affect data handling. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
