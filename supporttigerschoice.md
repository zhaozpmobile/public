- App version

## FAQ

### Does Tiger's Choice require an account?

No. You can play without creating an account.

### Can I play offline?

Yes. Core gameplay works offline.

### How do I reset game progress?

Use the reset option in the in-app settings.

### Why are there ads in the game?

Ads help support ongoing development and keep the game free.
